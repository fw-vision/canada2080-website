## A founding conversation

Canada2080 will establish a public long-horizon agenda for building the economic sovereignty, strategic capability, and public value Canada will need by 2080. The conversation begins with Francis Wang's Five Canadian Systemic Gaps and moves toward a preferred trajectory, its failure paths, and practical 90-day commitments.

The kickoff is designed for a small cross-sector room of policy leaders, investors, bankers, researchers, institutions, operators, community leaders, and selected media. Attendance will be confirmed directly after responses are reviewed for relevance, representation, and room capacity.

## Programme throughline

Canada's future strength depends less on what we discover than on what we can sustain and govern.
