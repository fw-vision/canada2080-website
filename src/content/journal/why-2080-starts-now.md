Canada2080 uses a distant horizon to improve present decisions. The year 2080 is an aspiration and a scenario boundary. It gives Canada enough time to build capabilities that require decades of institutional learning, infrastructure, patient capital, and generational renewal.

The initiative begins with a practical diagnosis. Canada has strong research, talent, institutions, resources, and specialised systems. It does not consistently connect those strengths to Canadian-controlled production, operating knowledge, demand, decision rights, and reinvestment.

## A public trajectory

Canada2080 will make the preferred trajectory inspectable. The website will publish the Five Canadian Systemic Gaps, regional missions, evidence classes, research, indicators, events, and accountability updates. It will also show the offramps that could weaken the work, including metrics theatre, political-cycle capture, technology lock-in, and infrastructure without legitimacy.

## The first convening

The September 28 kickoff release will bring a small cross-sector group together to test the agenda. The two-hour format combines networking, a focused one-hour programme, independent academic perspectives, and commitment-focused follow-up.

The launch asks each participant to identify one useful contribution and one possible 90-day action. Canada2080 will record interest carefully and will seek permission before presenting any view as an endorsement or commitment.

## What follows

The journal will hold factual event records, consented photographs, discussion syntheses, reflections, research interpretations, and progress reports. The first accountability test arrives 30 days after the kickoff: what moved, who owns the next step, and what evidence changed the plan.
