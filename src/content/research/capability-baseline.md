Canada2080 treats public research as an accountable record rather than a stream of conclusions. Each entry identifies what is known, which conditions qualify a claim, and what work remains.

The site will distinguish verified facts, qualified claims, scenarios, hypotheses, aspirations, and research needed. This classification helps readers assess a statement before acting on it.

Future entries will include named primary and authoritative sources, publication dates, definitions, geography, and limits. Publication remains subject to editorial, source, consent, and sensitivity review.
