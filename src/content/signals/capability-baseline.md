Canada2080 treats public research as an accountable record rather than a stream of conclusions. Each entry identifies what is known, which conditions qualify a claim, and what work remains.

Canada possesses substantial research and educational capability. Statistics Canada recorded $51.7 billion in gross domestic research and development expenditure in 2022, including $18.1 billion performed by higher education. The Council of Canadian Academies describes higher education as a comparative bright spot while identifying fragmentation, low business R&D, weak adoption, and declining performance in other parts of the system.

The resulting question is not whether Canada innovates. It is whether research, talent, rights, capital, demand, production, and reinvestment can form complete systems that compound value and public capability in Canada.

## A visible evidence standard

Signal entries distinguish verified facts, qualified claims, scenarios, hypotheses, aspirations, and research needed. Sources, dates, geography, definitions, and material limitations should remain available to the reader.

Publication remains subject to source, consent, privacy, Indigenous and cultural sensitivity, and public-consequence review.
